About Me

Football, Education &

Development

I’m Oussama Haddaji, a football coach and physical education professional focused on developing young players through structured technical, tactical and physical training. My approach combines discipline, teamwork and continuous progression to help every player improve both individually and within the group.

01.What is my coaching approach?
I believe player development comes from structured training, clear objectives and consistent progress. My sessions combine technical work, tactical understanding and physical preparation while encouraging discipline, responsibility and teamwork.
02.Who do I work with?
My experience is mainly focused on coaching and supporting young players, helping them develop their abilities, prepare for competition and progress according to their individual level and needs.
03.What areas do I focus on?
My work focuses on technical and tactical development, physical preparation, speed and coordination, along with planning training sessions and monitoring player performance.
04.What do I bring to the team?
I bring a combination of football coaching and physical education, with an emphasis on communication, group management, discipline and creating a positive environment where players can continue to develop.
